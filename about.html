About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery brings neighbors together over handmade food. Our goal is a welcoming stop for everyday treats and the special occasions that matter to our community.

Our sourcing philosophy

We value carefully chosen ingredients and seasonal variety. Ask our team about ingredient information before ordering, especially if you have a food allergy.

Meet the people behind the counter

Our bakers prepare the breads and treats. Our counter team helps neighbors choose items and organize pickup requests. Together, we aim to make each visit warm and welcoming.
